Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about traditional acupressure sessions, expectations, and clinic appointments.

Acupressure is a traditional, non-invasive complementary wellness discipline rooted in Eastern holistic traditions. It involves applying deliberate, manual finger or palm pressure to specific points across the body (known as acupoints or trigger points) to release muscular tension, improve natural circulation, and encourage overall bodily relaxation.
During a session, you remain fully clothed while resting comfortably on a treatment table. The practitioner applies gentle to firm pressure using fingers, thumbs, or palms on selected points. You may feel a deep ache, warmth, or releasing sensation as tense muscles relax. The pressure is always adjusted to your personal comfort level.

The number of sessions varies depending on your individual wellness goals, how long you have experienced discomfort, and your body's response. While many clients experience a sense of ease after a single session, a series of complementary sessions may be discussed during your consultation.
Acupressure is gentle and non-invasive, making it suitable for many adults. However, suitability can vary. Individuals who are pregnant, have severe osteoporosis, bleeding disorders, recent fractures, or active medical conditions should consult their qualified medical doctor prior to receiving acupressure.
